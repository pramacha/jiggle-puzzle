Privacy Policy — Jiggle Puzzle

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Jiggle Puzzle ("the app") is made by Prasanna R ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles information. The short version: we don't collect any personal information, and nothing you create leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access so you can scan a picture and turn it into a puzzle. Scanning and puzzle creation happen entirely on your device. Scanned images and puzzles are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or shared with anyone.

You can delete any puzzle inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Third-party services

  • iOS: document scanning uses Apple's built-in VisionKit, which runs on the device.
  • Android: document scanning uses Google's ML Kit through Google Play services. The scan is processed on the device, but Google Play services may send Google limited, non-identifying diagnostic and usage information about the scanning feature. See Google's privacy policy.

The app contains no other third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and their families. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, we do not collect, use, or disclose children's personal information as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The app has no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no links that lead out of the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change takes effect. If we ever begin collecting information from children, we will obtain verifiable parental consent first, as required by law.
